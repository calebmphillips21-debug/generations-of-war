Common questions

I bought a skin and it's missing. Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account you used to buy it.

How do I unlock more troops? Earn War Medals by playing, then spend them in the Armory. In Blitz you can also tap a locked card twice to unlock it during a battle.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads. Remove Ads turns off the ads shown between games. The "watch an ad to continue" offer in Blitz is optional and stays available. If ads between games still appear, tap Restore Purchases in the Shop.

My score isn't on the leaderboard. Check that you're signed in to Game Center in your device's Settings. Scores are sent when a Blitz run ends.
