Generations of War Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Generations of War is a strategy game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information is involved when you play it.

What the game stores

Your progress, such as War Medals, unlocked troops, trophies, high scores, campaign progress and settings, is saved on your own device. We do not run our own servers, we do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not receive this information.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your Blitz scores are sent to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards, under your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can manage it in your device's Settings.

In-app purchases

Optional skins, backgrounds and Remove Ads are bought through Apple. Apple handles your payment, and we never see your payment details.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional rewarded video ads, which you can choose to watch to continue a Blitz run, and full-screen ads between Campaign missions and Classic matches. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between games. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and how you interact with an ad, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. We do not ask for permission to track you, and ads in the game are not personalized. Learn more in Google's privacy policy and at how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your information

Because your progress is stored on your device, deleting the app removes it. Scores on Game Center are managed by Apple.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
